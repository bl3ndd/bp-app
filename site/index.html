7-day home check

One reading says little. A week taken the same way says a lot.

Home readings jump around. Before a diagnosis or a change in medication, guidelines ask for a week of readings taken in a set routine. Most people have never heard of it. Simply BP runs it for you.

Get ready

A short checklist: no coffee or exercise before, sit with your back supported, cuff on a bare arm.

Rest 5 minutes

A timer counts down, so the first reading isn't taken in a hurry.

Two readings

One, then another a minute later. Morning and evening, every day for a week.

One average

Day 1 is left out, as the guidelines advise. Days 2–7 become your home average.

Questions

Good to know

Does Simply BP measure my blood pressure?

No. No phone app can measure blood pressure with its camera or sensors. Measure with your home monitor, then type in the numbers, or let them come in from Apple Health.

Which monitors does it work with?

Any. You can type in readings from any cuff. If your monitor's app saves to Apple Health (Omron, Withings and many others do), readings appear in Simply BP automatically.

Do I have to do the 7-day check?

No. It's there when your doctor asks for a home average. Day to day, Simply BP works as a regular diary.

Where are my readings stored?

On your iPhone and, if you turn it on, in Apple Health. There's no account and no server copy. Anonymous usage counts help us improve the app and never include health data. See the privacy policy.

I bought Pro. How do I get it on a new phone?

Settings › Restore Purchases, with the same Apple Account. More answers on the support page.

Simply BP is not a medical device and doesn't give medical advice. Talk to your doctor about your readings. If you have a very high reading with chest pain, shortness of breath, weakness or trouble speaking, call 911 or your local emergency number.
