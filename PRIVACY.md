# BP Log — Privacy Policy (draft)

Draft to host at the URL in `BPLog/Models/AppLinks.swift` and enter in App Store Connect (guideline 5.1.1(i)).

_Last updated: October 30, 2026_

**Short version: BP Log doesn't collect your data. Your readings stay on your devices and in Apple Health. We never see them.**

## What the app stores
The blood pressure readings you enter (top and bottom number, pulse, date and time, arm, position, tags and notes) are stored only on your device. BP Log has no account, no servers, no analytics and no advertising, and it makes no network requests of its own.

## Apple Health
If you turn on Apple Health in Settings, BP Log:
- **writes** the readings you enter to Apple Health as blood pressure and heart rate samples;
- **reads** blood pressure and heart rate samples from Apple Health, including ones written by your blood pressure monitor's app.

Health data is used only to show your readings, statistics and reports in the app. It is never shared with anyone, never used for advertising or marketing, and never stored in iCloud by BP Log. Apple Health's own syncing between your devices is managed by Apple under your iCloud settings. You can turn off access at any time in the iOS Settings app › Health › Data Access & Devices › BP Log.

## Backups and reports
When you tap "Back Up Readings" or create a PDF report, the app creates a file and you choose where to save or send it. BP Log does not upload these files anywhere.

## Purchases
Purchases are handled by Apple. We don't receive your payment details.

## Deleting your data
Delete individual readings in History, or delete the app to remove all data stored by it. Readings already saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app. We hold no copy, so there is nothing to request from us.

## Children
BP Log is not directed at children under 13.
